Process

How I work through every data challenge.

01
Understand
Start with the business question — not the data. Clarify what decision needs to be made and what would count as a useful answer.
02
Extract & Clean
Query, collect, and rigorously clean the data. Bad inputs mean bad insights — this step is where reliability is built.
03
Analyse & Model
Explore patterns, build models, and surface the findings that matter — filtering out noise to highlight what drives the outcome.
04
Communicate
Package insights into dashboards, reports, or presentations — designed for the audience, not the analyst.
